No data collected

Ladon does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

On your device

Your best score, your constellation and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score goes to the leaderboard through Apple's Game Center service. The shape of your dragon travels along with it, so other players can see your constellation and your Game Center name in their sky. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive it.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline.
