Need help?

Email appsupport@mariusvandam.com. If something does not work, mention your iPhone or iPad model and its iOS version.

How do I steer?

Swipe in the direction you want to go. Swipe against your direction for a tight U-turn. With a keyboard you steer with the arrow keys or WASD.

What do the golden apples, combos and arrows do?

  • A golden apple is worth 50 points and makes you younger: three pieces shorter. It disappears after 7.5 seconds.
  • Eat again within four seconds for a combo: your points count ×2, ×3, up to ×5.
  • After every four fruits an arrow of Heracles falls from the sky and stays in the board. An orange glow warns you first.

How do I look around, or keep the camera still?

  • On the title screen, swipe sideways to turn the camera around the island. Swipe up or down to lower or raise it.
  • During a game, tap the camera button at the bottom right. The view then stays still. Tap again to let it move.
  • Is Reduce Motion on for your device? Then the camera is fixed from the start.

I hear no sound

Check the sound button at the bottom right of the screen during a game, and the volume of your device.

I don't see the Leaderboard button or the constellations of other players

Both appear once you are signed in to Game Center: Settings → Game Center.
